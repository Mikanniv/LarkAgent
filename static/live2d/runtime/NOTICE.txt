The WebAssembly runtime includes adler2 2.0.1, miniz_oxide 0.8.9,
ruzstd 0.8.3 and twox-hash 2.1.5. Their license notices are retained
in the licenses directory. Crate checksums were verified against Cargo.lock.

This is an independent player for authored layered mesh rigs.
It does not include official Live2D Cubism Core or SDK binaries.
